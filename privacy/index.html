Privacy Policy

StorageCleaner

Effective Date: April 2026

StorageCleaner is designed to process your photos, contacts, and storage insights on your device where possible.

This Privacy Policy explains how StorageCleaner handles information when you use the app.

1. Information the app can access

The app may request permission to access the following data so features can work as intended:

  • Photos library for duplicate review, similar photo grouping, screenshot cleanup, blurry photo review, video compression, and photo search.
  • Contacts for duplicate or incomplete contact review.
  • Local device storage information to show storage breakdowns and reclaimable space.

2. How data is used

Data accessed by the app is used only to provide cleanup, review, search, compression, and storage insight features inside the app.

3. On-device processing

StorageCleaner is built to perform analysis on-device where possible. This includes tasks such as duplicate detection, similarity grouping, and local search preparation.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. We do not share your photos or contacts for marketing purposes.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment details or Apple ID credentials.

6. Your choices

You can revoke Photos or Contacts access at any time in iOS Settings. Some features may stop working until access is restored.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.

8. Contact
