Your iPhone Storage Is Full Again. Stunning. Truly Shocking.

Your iPhone says storage is full, which is adorable because apparently the 19 blurry screenshots of delivery codes, three identical concert videos, and that one 4K clip of your shoe all needed a forever home. Storage Cleaner exists for this exact little disaster: it helps you find the junk, review it like an adult, and delete only what you actually meant to delete. Radical concept, I know.

Start with the stuff that got huge quietly.

Large videos are usually the villain wearing a fake mustache. One "quick" clip can eat more space than a hundred normal photos, and iOS will politely let that happen while you wonder why your phone can no longer download a 43 MB update.

Do not trust blind deletion.

Duplicate and similar photos should be reviewed before cleanup. The almost-identical photo might be useless, or it might be the one where everyone has their eyes open. Software cannot always know. You can. Probably.

The useful cleanup habit.

Open Storage Cleaner, scan the obvious categories, review before deleting, and stop when the phone has enough breathing room. You do not need to become a minimalist. You just need your camera to work at dinner.
