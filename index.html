FAQ

Questions users ask before they download.

Keep the final objections small, clear, and honest. The answers below are designed to reduce hesitation without overpromising.

Does StorageCleaner upload my photos or contacts?
No. The landing page and product positioning are built around on-device AI processing. Your library stays on your iPhone rather than being uploaded to a cloud service for analysis.
Will the app delete anything automatically?
No. StorageCleaner follows a review-before-delete workflow. You scan, inspect the results, and decide what to remove.
Why do I still need to empty Recently Deleted?
iOS keeps deleted photos and videos in Photos > Recently Deleted for a recovery period. To finish freeing space, open Photos > Recently Deleted, choose Select, then Delete All.
What can I do for free?
In the current live App Store version, the core cleanup flow is available without a purchase, including scans, review, video compression, and contacts cleanup.
Do I need to buy anything to use the app right now?
No. The current live version is free-first and does not require a purchase to use the core cleanup flow.
Why does the app need Photos and Contacts permissions?
Photos access powers storage analysis, duplicate and similar-photo review, screenshot cleanup, blurry photo detection, and video workflows. Contacts access is only needed if you choose to use contacts cleanup.
Which devices are supported?
StorageCleaner is built for iPhone and currently requires iOS 17 or later.
Do I need an account to use it?
No account is required to get started.
