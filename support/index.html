Troubleshooting

If a feature is not working as expected, check the following first:

  • Make sure Photos or Contacts permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Keep the device unlocked and charging for longer indexing tasks.
  • Re-open the app after changing permissions.
  • Use the in-app diagnostics screen if a scan appears stuck.

Safety and privacy

  • StorageCleaner is designed so AI processing happens on-device.
  • Your library is not uploaded to cloud servers for core AI analysis.
  • Nothing is deleted automatically. Every cleanup suggestion is reviewed first.
